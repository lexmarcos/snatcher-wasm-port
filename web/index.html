Snatcher

Play your own copy of Snatcher (USA, Sega CD) in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

1Your disc

Pick the .cue file and its .bin file(s) together, or drop them here. Release to import

On a phone or tablet: tap Import disc image, then pick the .cue and its .bin file(s) in the list (iPhone: tap Select first).

Your files are read in this browser tab only and are never uploaded. The imported data stays in this browser until you remove it.

2Play

Import your disc in step 1, then press Play.

    Other ways to load

    Already have the game files extracted? Choose that folder (the project's assets folder works best). The files are read in this page only.

    Developers: after the extraction, bun web/server.ts serves ./assets and this page selects it by itself.

    Remove the files and clear the site

    Remove only the imported disc: your saves and settings stay. Import the disc again to play.

    Clear the site: removes the imported disc, your saves, gamepad buttons and settings from this browser. Export your saves first to keep them.
